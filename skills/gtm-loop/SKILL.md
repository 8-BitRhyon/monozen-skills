# GTM Loop

> Distribution is earned before launch day. The launch amplifies an existing loop; it does not create one.

## Contract

- Input: `MARKETING.md`, `POSITIONING.md`, activation data.
- Output: `LAUNCH.md` and `GROWTH.md`.
- Stop: one measured decision per week, with a baseline and a stop rule.

## 1. Proof Before Traffic

- 5-10 design partners, paid pilot or LOI.
- One activation event, instrumented.
- Quickstart under 5 minutes; time five strangers.
- 90-second demo, outcome first.
- Three proof assets: benchmark, before/after, named customer with a number.
- Honest comparison page and one migration guide.
- Pricing with a visible number.

## 2. Six-Week Launch

| Week | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proof | 10 partner calls, 5 signed, ICP one-pager |
| 2 | Build in public | Daily changelog, 3 answer pages, demo |
| 3 | Private beta | 20-50 users, activation funnel, 3 testimonials |
| 4 | Community | 2 existing communities, 1 newsletter swap, 5 pitches |
| 5 | Social proof | Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn same day |
| 6 | HN and review | Show HN, 24h reply sprint, postmortem |

One spike per week. Never run two.

## 3. Channel Sequence

Owned list -> niche communities -> Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn -> Hacker News -> YouTube and comparison content.

Advance only when the previous step converts. Product Hunt is a badge, not a pipeline. Show HN is a pulse: median 2 points, half the attention gone in about 7 hours. YouTube compounds slowest and correlates most with AI visibility.

## 4. Weekly Growth Loop

```
discover -> first run -> habit -> artifact -> teammate -> team buys
```

- Activation: first run on a real repo, no sample project, under 5 minutes.
- Retention: measure week 4, not daily.
- Referral: the committed config or artifact is the referral unit.
- Expansion: seats, then pooled usage, then policy, SSO, audit.

## 5. Metrics And Stop Rules

Set a baseline before a target. Track:

- time to first success, under 5 minutes
- activation: 3 successful runs in 14 days, around 30%
- week-4 retained use, around 40%
- free to paid, 5-8%
- net revenue retention, above 100%

Stop rules:

- Activation under 30% at 48 hours: stop launching, fix onboarding.
- Day-7 retention under 10% for the launch cohort: no new channels for two weeks.
- Two channels under 10 qualified signups: 20 customer interviews.
- A page with no AI citations and under 10 clicks at 60 days: delete or merge.
- Never re-launch the same product on the same platform inside six months.

## Anti-Patterns

- A waitlist treated as demand.
- Product Hunt as the pipeline.
- Paid ads before activation works.
- Programmatic AI content at scale.
- Vanity metrics: stars, impressions, signups, upvotes.
- A DevRel hire before the quickstart works.
