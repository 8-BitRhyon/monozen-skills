## Proof Checklist

- [ ] 5-10 design partners, paid pilot or LOI
- [ ] Activation event instrumented
- [ ] Quickstart under 5 minutes, timed with 5 strangers
- [ ] 90-second demo, outcome first
- [ ] Benchmark or before/after with a number
- [ ] Named customer or design partner quote
- [ ] Honest comparison page
- [ ] Pricing with a visible number
- [ ] Ten objection replies drafted
